Our story

From a plot of land to a growing academy

Eagles Academy began with a simple idea from Eng. Samuel Okurut Tukei: secure the land first, then build. Classroom blocks went up in phases, starting with Pre-primary and expanding one level at a time through P1 to P7 — with teaching starting almost as soon as each block was ready.

The school sits on plots 17–19, Dr. Aporu Okol Road, and plots 2–4, Opejo Road, in Kumi Town — over ten hectares in total, giving pupils a calm, uncrowded place to learn and the school room to grow.

The plan doesn't stop at Primary Seven. With support from partners and well-wishers, Eagles Academy is working toward becoming a full-cycle institution offering secondary education and beyond.
